EZEE DRIVING SCHOOL - AUTOMATIC BOOKING EMAIL SETUP

Upload these files to the SAME folder on your hosting:
- index.html
- booking.php

How it works:
- The booking form does NOT open the visitor's email app.
- Booking details are sent automatically to ezeedrivingschool@yahoo.com.
- The client receives an automatic acknowledgement email.
- The visitor sees a professional confirmation message on the website.

Hosting requirements:
- PHP support
- PHP mail() enabled

IMPORTANT:
Some hosting providers disable or restrict PHP mail(). For reliable delivery, ask your host to enable outgoing mail, or configure authenticated SMTP with your domain email service.
