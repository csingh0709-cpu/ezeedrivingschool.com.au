VORT — Vehicle On Road Test

Everything you need to understand the South Australian VORT pathway.

What is VORT?

The Vehicle On Road Test is one of the practical pathways to a P1 provisional licence in South Australia. A qualified authorised examiner assesses your driving skills, vehicle operation, observation, hazard management and interaction with other road users.

  • Approximately 45-minute practical test
  • Five low-speed manoeuvres
  • General drive in real traffic
  • Pass mark of 90% or more, with no road-law breach

Requirements and government rules can change. Always confirm current requirements with the South Australian Government.

VORT preparation pathway

Complete your learner requirements and supervised driving hours.
Build your skills with a qualified driving instructor and/or supervising driver.
Practise the five slow-speed manoeuvres and general driving skills.
Book your VORT with an authorised examiner when you are ready.
Complete the test and, if successful, receive your Certificate of Competency.
